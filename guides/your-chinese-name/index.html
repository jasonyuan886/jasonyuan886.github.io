The secret behind a real Chinese name

In China, a name is never just a pretty sound. It's born from your Bazi (八字, "Eight Characters") — the year, month, day and hour of your birth, mapped onto the Five Elements (五行):

木Woodgrowth · creativity
火Firepassion · warmth
土Earthstability · care
金Metalprecision · clarity
水Waterwisdom · depth
🔥 The elements feed each other in a cycle — Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal carries Water, Water nourishes Wood. When your chart runs heavy on one element or light on another, traditional naming adds the element you're missing through the characters themselves. Spoken every day for a lifetime, your name becomes a quiet wish — a daily rebalance of who you are.
