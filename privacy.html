Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

ChinaPal AI ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Shenzhen Qili Technology Co., Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our translation and travel assistant service at aihubai.cn.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide our services:

  • Chat records: Conversations with our AI assistant, including text queries and responses.
  • Photo data: Images you upload for translation or recognition purposes. Photos are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored.
  • Account information: Email address and PayPal transaction ID when you subscribe to a paid plan.
  • Usage data: App usage frequency, feature interactions, and device type.
  • Location data: General location for travel recommendations (optional, with your consent).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide real-time translation and travel assistance.
  • Process image translations (menus, signs, documents).
  • Manage your subscription and payment records.
  • Improve service quality and AI model accuracy.
  • Send important service updates (no marketing without consent).

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on encrypted servers. Chat logs are retained for 30 days to provide context for ongoing conversations, then automatically deleted. Photo data is processed in memory and not stored after the session ends. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and preferences. Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • DeepSeek API: Powers our AI translation engine. Data sent to DeepSeek is processed in compliance with their data handling policies and is not used for model training.
  • PayPal: Handles all payment processing. We do not store your credit card or banking details.
  • Cloud hosting providers: We use secure cloud infrastructure for service delivery.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data we hold.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. Upon deletion request, all personal data is removed within 30 days. Payment records are retained for 2 years for tax and legal compliance.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be notified via email or in-app notice. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
